About

My interest in combining design and business is not a coincidence, but the result of a clearly developed path. As a product developer, I learned how an idea grows step by step into a well-considered, functional and appealing product.

A strong design background alone is not enough to make an impact in the market. Understanding the business, client relationships and commercial strategy matters just as much. That is why I am now studying Product Management & UX Design, to connect both worlds fluently.

What shaped me

The car design course at IED permanently changed how I look at design. Daniele Mazzon, then head of transportation and yacht design, showed me how form, engineering and brand identity come together in one product, and how every design decision can be traced back to the wishes and experience of the end client. That way of working is still a lasting source of inspiration.

What drives me

I don't just want to watch a design idea come to life. I want to help that idea find its way to the market and to the people who use and experience it, through client presentations, proposals, market research and business development.
