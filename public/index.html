About

From designing products to bringing them to market

My interest in combining design and business is not a coincidence, but the result of a clearly developed path. As a product developer, I learned how an idea grows step by step into a well-considered, functional and appealing product.

The car design course at IED permanently changed how I look at design. Daniele Mazzon, then head of transportation and yacht design, showed me how form, engineering and brand identity come together in one product, and how every design decision can be traced back to the end client.

A strong design background alone is not enough to make an impact in the market. Understanding the business, client relationships and commercial strategy matters just as much.

That is why I am now studying Product Management & UX Design: to connect both worlds fluently. My logo says the same thing. The arc is the bridge from product to business; the V is the person who carries it.
