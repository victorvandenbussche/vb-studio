My story

I started as an industrial product designer: Howest, a summer of car design at IED Turin, an Erasmus semester at Chalmers. But every experience taught me the same thing: a great product only matters if it finds its market.

So I moved toward the commercial side. I started an MSc in Product Management & UX Design at SKEMA and Politecnico di Milano, and in summer 2026 I put it into practice as a Business Development Intern at Sumum.
