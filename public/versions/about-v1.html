From product design to the market.

My story

I'm an industrial product designer. At Howest I learned how an idea grows step by step into a functional, appealing product, and a car design summer school at IED in Turin showed me how form, engineering and brand identity come together around the end client.

My internships taught me the other half: a strong design alone doesn't make an impact in the market. Understanding the business, the client and the strategy matters just as much. That is why I am now studying Product Management & UX Design at SKEMA Business School and Politecnico di Milano.

The next step is to cross that bridge in practice. I am looking for an internship or apprenticeship in product management, where I can bring a designer's mindset to business decisions. Let's talk →
