The bridge from product to business

The arc spans, the V carries: the core of product management.

From product design to the market

I'm an industrial product designer. At Howest I learned how an idea grows step by step into a functional, appealing product, and a car design summer school at IED in Turin showed me how form, engineering and brand identity come together around the end client.

My internships taught me the other half. At Sumum I supported product management with cost price analysis and competitor benchmarking, and presented a new range to customers in Copenhagen. A strong design alone doesn't make an impact in the market: understanding the business, the client and the strategy matters just as much.

That is why I am now studying Product Management & UX Design at SKEMA Business School and Politecnico di Milano, to connect both worlds fluently.

The next step is to cross that bridge in practice. I am looking for an internship or apprenticeship in product management, where I can bring a designer's mindset to business decisions. Let's talk →
