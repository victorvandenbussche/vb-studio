Analogue clockShaped after a piano

A clock in the design language of a celebrity. I chose Elton John and shaped it after his piano.

What
Analogue table clock, within 25 × 25 × 25 cm
Where
Howest, Industrial Product Design, 2022
Role
Individual project, from persona to prototype

The brief

Design an analogue clock in the design language of a celebrity, within 25 × 25 × 25 cm. I chose Elton John and based the clock on his grand piano. His style gave me my rules: special edges, bright colours, and a mix of hard, sharp and round lines.

From sketch to foam

A form perception canvas helped me place the clock between opposites, such as playful or serious and soft or hard. Then I sketched shapes that start square at the clock face and flow into the curve of a piano, and carved them in foam to compare them side by side.

The result

The final shape is a square clock face that grows into the curved body of a grand piano. I modelled and rendered it in CAD, then built a painted prototype to check the proportions in real life.
