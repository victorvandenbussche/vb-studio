Two products I designed on my own: the problem, how I tackled it, and what came out.

MyRamp

Designing for independence · Bachelor thesis · My Add On · 2024

  • ProblemKerbs and steps stop active wheelchair users from going where they want, on their own.
  • ApproachConcepts, prototypes and user testing, iterated with the INNOWIZ method: ergonomics, folding, handles, storage.
  • ResultA light, foldable aluminium ramp, tested with users. Thesis graded 16/20.

Backplate

Designing for the individual · Parametric design · 3D printing

  • ProblemBack supports come in standard sizes, while every back is different.
  • ApproachA 3D scan of the back, turned into a parametric model in Rhino and shaped to that one body.
  • ResultA personalised, 3D-printed back support with an open-cell structure.

Analogue clock

Designing in someone else's style · Individual project · Howest · 2022

  • ProblemDesign an analogue clock in the design language of a celebrity, within 25 × 25 × 25 cm.
  • ApproachElton John's piano as the starting point: a form perception canvas, sketches and five foam models.
  • ResultA red clock whose face grows into the curve of a grand piano, rendered and prototyped.

Low-cost prosthesis

Designing on a tight budget · Group project · Howest · 2023

  • ProblemA prosthesis for the African market had to cost under €50 and be made without 3D printing.
  • ApproachThree concepts, from a bicycle saddle as a foot to a seat post that grows with the user.
  • ResultOne prototype with a damping wooden foot, an adjustable leg and a socket for the knee stump.
