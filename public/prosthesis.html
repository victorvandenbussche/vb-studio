Low-cost prosthesisA leg for under €50

A lower-leg prosthesis for the African market: cheap, made without 3D printing, and able to grow with its user.

What
Below-knee prosthesis for under €50
Where
Howest, Industrial Design Center, 2023
Role
Group project, in a team of three

The brief

Design a prosthesis for the African market. It had to cost less than €50, be made locally, so no 3D printing, and meet all the basic requirements of a prosthesis. Our list of demands: support, absorb, adjust, be kind to the environment, and last.

Three concepts

We built three prototypes, each testing a different answer.

The final prototype

We brought the best of the three concepts together in one prototype, built from three parts:

  • The foot damps each step and springs back.
  • The leg can be set to the right length and grows with the user.
  • The socket attaches to the knee stump.
