MyRampDesigning for independence

A portable ramp that helps active wheelchair users get over kerbs and steps on their own. My bachelor thesis, and the project that pointed me towards product management.

What
Mobile, lightweight ramp for active wheelchair users
Where
My Add On, Jan – Jun 2024
Role
Individual project · Bachelor thesis, graded 16/20

The project

MyRamp is a mobile, compact and lightweight ramp for active wheelchair users. I developed it during my internship at My Add On, as my bachelor thesis in Industrial Product Design. The challenge was simple but meaningful: help wheelchair users overcome everyday obstacles and keep their independence.

I took it from concepts and sketches to prototypes, user testing and design iterations, and worked on the ramp's ergonomics, folding mechanism, handles, storage system and user guidance. Every decision had to make the product more practical, intuitive and accessible.

What it taught me

At first my enthusiasm pushed me straight into ideas and prototypes. User feedback and the INNOWIZ design method taught me to work more systematically: question my assumptions, and look for the right solution for the people who use it, not the fastest one. Testing, listening and iterating became the core of my process.

It also showed me what I was missing: how a product reaches the market, and how its value becomes real for its users. I became more and more interested in user needs, product vision and the story that connects a product to real-world value.

I went from wanting to design great products to wanting to understand how great products create value, reach people and become meaningful parts of their lives.

Why Product Management

I want to help shape what gets built, why it gets built and how it creates value, connecting user needs, business goals and product development. Today I bring a designer's mindset to that: curiosity, empathy, a hands-on attitude and a strong wish to understand people.
