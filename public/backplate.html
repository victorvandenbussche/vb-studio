Project · Product design

BackplateDesigning for the individual

An individual product design project exploring personalisation through parametric design: a product that adapts to the human body.

What
Personalised back support for better posture
How
3D scan, parametric design in Rhino, 3D printing
Role
Individual project, from problem to final product

The challenge

Poor sitting habits and long hours leaning forward cause discomfort. Backplate is a back support that encourages better posture. The challenge was to design a parametric product that offers a high degree of personalisation: instead of one size for everyone, a product that fits one person.

The process

  1. A 3D scan of the user's back.
  2. The scan becomes a digital model in Rhino.
  3. Parametric design adapts the backplate's shape to that body.
  4. The final piece is made by 3D printing.
Products should adapt to people, rather than expecting people to adapt to products.

My role

I took ownership of the whole design process: understanding the problem, developing the concept, working out the technical workflow and creating the final product. Working alone meant making my own design decisions and turning an initial idea into something tangible.

What interests me most is the link between technology and personalisation: how digital tools can move product design beyond standard solutions, towards products that respond to individual needs.

What this project shows

  • OwnershipTaking a project from first problem to tangible result on my own.
  • Creative problem-solvingLooking past standard solutions to what one person actually needs.
  • Technical understandingHow 3D scanning, parametric modelling and 3D printing work together.
  • User-oriented thinkingDesigning around the person who will use the product.
