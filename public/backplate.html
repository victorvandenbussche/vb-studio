BackplateDesigning for the individual

A back support shaped to one person's body, from a 3D scan to a 3D-printed product.

What
Personalised back support for better posture
How
3D scan, parametric design in Rhino, 3D printing
Role
Individual project, from problem to final product

The challenge

Poor posture, from long hours leaning forward at a desk or carrying a heavy bag, causes back discomfort. Backplate is a back support that encourages better posture. The challenge was to design a parametric product that offers a high degree of personalisation: instead of one size for everyone, a product that fits one person.

The process

  1. A 3D scan of the user's back.
  2. The scan becomes a digital model in Rhino.
  3. Parametric design adapts the backplate's shape to that body.
  4. The final piece is made by 3D printing.
Products should adapt to people, rather than expecting people to adapt to products.

My role

I took full ownership of the whole process: understanding the problem, developing the concept, working out the technical workflow and creating the final product, making my own design decisions along the way.

It taught me how 3D scanning, parametric modelling and 3D printing work together.
